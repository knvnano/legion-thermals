LEGION THERMALS — INSTALLATION AND QUICK START

Windows10/11 x64. The installer includes.NET10 Desktop Runtime.

1. Close the previous version through its tray menu → Exit, then install.
2. A normal launch requests administrator access immediately through Windows UAC.
3. Use the language button at the top of Settings to switch Русский / English.
4. Enable elevated startup in Settings to create a Windows Task Scheduler task.
   It starts the app10seconds after sign-in with highest privileges, using your
   interactive token and no stored password. No repeated UAC prompt at sign-in.
5. Edit the temperature/speed table and enable the temperature scheduler.
   It uses max(CPU,GPU), polls every2seconds and accepts30–100% in10% steps.
   Cooling down has a3°C hysteresis. CPU≥90°C or GPU≥80°C forces maximum cooling.
6. Manual/table percentages are a MINIMUM curve floor, not a maximum RPM cap.
   Measured RPM and firmware target minimum are displayed separately.

Use Lenovo Auto or Exit to restore the original curve, mode and full-speed flag.
Forced termination defers journal recovery until the next administrator launch.
Settings and language are saved automatically in your local user profile.
Drag the banner to move it; change opacity and visibility in Settings.

Fan writes are enabled only for Lenovo83DF / N0CN BIOS. Other models can differ.
The app does not include a hardware driver or third-party sensor library.
